# Wiba Signals

**Decision-grade intelligence from primary sources.**

Wiba Signals synthesizes large volumes of mixed-source data — academic studies, primary reports, transcripts, and live market data — into sharp, human-readable intelligence for decision-makers.

---

## Editorial Standards

Every published figure must meet three conditions before it appears on Wiba Signals:

1. **Source traceability** — The claim must be traceable to a named primary or secondary source.
2. **Numerical precision** — Exact figures are cited exactly. Approximations are labelled as such.
3. **Contextual accuracy** — No figure may be isolated from its methodological context.

Full methodology: [wibasignals.com/methodology/](https://wibasignals.com/methodology/)
